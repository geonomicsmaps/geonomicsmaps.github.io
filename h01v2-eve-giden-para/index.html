Which Country Gets Money From Workers Abroad Worth Over Half Its GDP?

Geonomics. Every narrated claim with its source and a short quote in the original language. Errors? Say so in the video's comments; corrections are pinned.

Narration

One country on this map got money from workers abroad worth over half its economy last year.

  • World Bank BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS, 2025

India gets the most of anyone, about 150 billion dollars a year.

  • World Bank BX.TRF.PWKR.CD.DT, 2025

But India's economy is so big, that's only 3.8 percent.

  • World Bank BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS, 2025

The Philippines gets a bigger slice. One dollar in five sent through banks comes from sailors.

  • World Bank, 2025
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, Table 11, 2025
    Land-based 28,494,756; Sea-based 7,139,028; Total 35,633,784 (bin $)

Then the share starts climbing, fast. Guatemala is at about a fifth of its economy, almost all of it from the United States.

  • World Bank, 2025
  • IDB, via LatinNews
    Guatemala (92.9%), and Honduras (90.1%)

Next door, Honduras leans on the same money for about 30 percent of its economy.

  • World Bank, 2025
  • El Heraldo, citing Banco Central de Honduras, 2025

Nepal is higher still, and most Nepalis leaving for new jobs head to the Gulf or Malaysia.

  • World Bank, 2025
  • Asia News Network, citing Nepal's Department of Foreign Employment, 2025
    Gulf countries still hold 55.4 percent ... Malaysia accounts for 15.9 percent

Then Tonga, a tiny Pacific kingdom, at 39 percent, from Tongan families abroad and seasonal workers.

  • World Bank, 2025
  • National Reserve Bank of Tonga, Monthly Economic Update (September), 2025
    continued support from the Tongan diaspora and active participation in the labour mobility schemes

But the country we were looking for is in the mountains of Central Asia. Tajikistan. 58 percent of what the country makes.

  • World Bank BX.TRF.PWKR.DT.GD.ZS, 2025

Sent home, that pay is worth about four times what the country earned selling goods to the world.

  • World Bank BX.TRF.PWKR.CD.DT + TX.VAL.MRCH.CD.WT, 2025

Most of those workers are in one country: Russia.

  • World Bank report, via The Times of Central Asia, 2023
    over 80% of migration from these countries directed there in 2023

Our calculations

  • 58 percent: the World Bank value for Tajikistan in 2025 is 57.66% of GDP, rounded.
  • About four times: Tajikistan received 10.2 billion dollars from workers abroad and exported 2.49 billion dollars of goods; 10.2 ÷ 2.49 = 4.1 (World Bank, 2025). Goods only; services exports are not included.
  • One dollar in five: cash remittances from sea-based workers through banks, 7.14 billion dollars, ÷ all cash remittances through banks, 35.63 billion dollars = 20.0% (Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, 2025, preliminary).
  • Rounded in the narration: Guatemala 20.8% ("about a fifth"), Honduras 30.1% ("about 30 percent"), Tonga 39.3% ("39 percent").

Worth knowing

  • The indicator is personal remittances received as a share of GDP: personal transfers plus compensation of employees (World Bank definition).
  • These are selected countries, not a full ranking. Among countries with a 2025 figure, Tajikistan was the only one above 50%; the next was Tonga at 39.3%. Some countries have no 2025 figure yet.
  • Philippines: 8.5% on screen is remittances as a share of GDP (World Bank). "One dollar in five" is a different measure: cash remittances through banks (BSP).
  • Guatemala: the United States share (92.9%) is an IDB figure reported by LatinNews; the year was not stated and we could not open the IDB report itself.
  • Honduras: the United States share is between 90.1% (IDB) and about 98.5% (Banco Central de Honduras, reported by El Heraldo).
  • Nepal: the Gulf (55.4%) and Malaysia (15.9%) shares are of new work permits in the 2025/26 fiscal year, not of all Nepalis working abroad.
  • Tajikistan: over 80% of migration went to Russia in 2023, according to a World Bank report as reported by The Times of Central Asia. We could not open the report itself, and we did not check how 2025 Russian restrictions changed this.
