Ares Medya Yayin

Ares Medya Yayin is a private tool. Its owner uses it to upload videos to, schedule videos on, and read analytics for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does with Google data: it uploads the owner's own video files, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, posts the channel's own first comment, and reads view and retention statistics of the owner's channels. Nothing is shared with anyone else.
