95% of Egyptians live on 4% of the land

Look at where Egyptians actually live, and you don't see a country. You see a river. About 95 percent of them live in a thin band along the Nile and its delta.

  • CIA World Factbook (open-data mirror), 2025
    approximately 95% of the population lives within 20 km (12 mi) of the Nile River and its delta; vast areas of the country remain sparsely populated or uninhabited

That band is roughly 4 percent of the land.

  • FAO AQUASTAT country profile, 2005
    a vast desert plateau interrupted by the Nile Valley and Delta which occupy about 4 percent of the total country area
  • Egypt State Information Service, 2026
    The Nile Valley and Delta constitute less than 4% of the total area of the Arab Republic of Egypt, representing about 33,000 km2.

It is also where most of the farmland is.

  • FAO AQUASTAT, 2002
    Most of the cultivated land is located close to the banks of the Nile River, its main branches and canals, and in the Nile Delta … The total …

Cairo sits inside that band, and its crowding is real. Its urban area holds more than 22 million people.

Our calculations

  • 1 trillion Egyptian pounds at the 2025 average rate: 1,000 ÷ 49.23 = 20.3 billion dollars (World Bank official rate). Reuters' 58 billion dollars implies about 17.2 pounds per dollar (1,000 ÷ 58).
  • 500 billion pounds at the 2025 average rate: 500 ÷ 49.23 = 10.2 billion dollars. Reuters gave 32 billion dollars before the 2022 devaluations and 16 billion dollars at the January 2024 rate. The money was spent over several years, so no single rate is exact. All figures are nominal, not adjusted for inflation.
  • Pound lost about two thirds against the dollar: 1 − 16.77 ÷ 49.23 = 66% (annual averages, 2019 to 2025). A dollar of debt costs 49.23 ÷ 16.77 = 2.9 times as many pounds.
  • The three loans: 0.843 + 1.364 + 0.800 = about 3.0 billion dollars (AidData).
  • Water: 1.5 million m³ a day × 365 = 547.5 million m³ a year; ÷ 55.5 billion m³ (Egypt's share of the Nile) = about 1%, matching Reuters.
  • Bar of residents versus plan: 30,000 ÷ 6.5 million = 0.46%. The 6.5 million is the plan for the finished city in three phases; only phase one is close to finished.

Worth knowing

  • The company's figures (residents, land sold, revenue, profit) come from the company or its chairman and were not independently audited in the sources we could open.
  • We could not confirm whether the 4 billion pounds a year in rent announced in 2022 has been paid since, or who repays the Chinese-led loans in the end.
  • The tower is 393.8 m to the tip according to the Council on Vertical Urbanism; Egypt's State Information Service lists 385 m. It was the tallest completed building in Africa as of November 2026; Tour F in Abidjan, 421 m to the tip, is expected to pass it.
  • 95% (CIA World Factbook: within 20 km of the Nile and its delta) and 4% (FAO, State Information Service: the Nile Valley and Delta) use slightly different definitions.
  • About 27% of the entries behind this video are primary documents; most of the money story rests on statements by officials reported by news agencies, and the narration says so where it matters.
