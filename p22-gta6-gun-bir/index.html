The cut you never see

Go back ten days before launch. Take-Two tells investors what to expect from October through December, for the entire company, GTA 6 included. The top of its forecast: about three and a half billion dollars.

  • Take-Two 8-K Ex.99.1 (Q2 FY2027), 9.11.2026, 2026
    Net Bookings $3,350 to $3,500

Then a single game's first day comes in above that.

  • G1 and G15 (our math), 2026

That sounds impossible. It isn't. The two numbers count different money. The first-day number is what players paid at the checkout. The forecast counts only what reaches Take-Two.

  • Take-Two 8-K Ex.99.1, 18.9.2013, 2013
    achieved first day sell-through of more than $800 million worldwide
  • Take-Two 8-K Ex.99.1 (Q2 FY2027), 9.11.2026, 2026
    the net amount of products and services sold digitally or sold-in physically during the period

And in between stands the store. On PlayStation and Xbox, Take-Two's annual report says it records only what the storefront passes on.

  • Take-Two 10-K FY2026 (31.3.2026), Note 1, revenue recognition, 2026
    we report revenue based on the consideration received from the digital storefront

So the store's cut never shows up as a cost. It's gone before the first line of the accounts. How big is it? Take-Two doesn't disclose it. But the standard console store rate, in a 2021 U.S. court ruling, was thirty percent.

  • U.S. District Court N.D. Cal., Epic Games v. Apple, 4:20-cv-05640-YGR, Dkt 812, 2021
    Epic Games has agreed to such a rate on all Fortnite transactions via the Microsoft (Xbox) Store, the PlayStation Store
  • Epic Games v. Apple, Dkt 812, 2021
    Most of these platforms, including Steam, charged a 30% commission.

Our calculations

  • "Roughly three billion": 32.5 million units sold before launch (Take-Two, Nov. 20, 2026) × $80 standard to $100 Ultimate Edition (U.S. list prices) ≈ $2.6-3.25 billion. Our math: prices differ by country and edition, taxes and currencies are ignored, and Take-Two has not said how its $3.79 billion splits between pre-release and launch-day sales.
  • "About two point seven is left": $3.79 billion − 30% ≈ $2.65 billion, against Take-Two's Oct-Dec 2026 net bookings forecast of $3.35-3.50 billion for the whole company (Nov. 9, 2026). Our math, only if the standard 30% console store rate applied to every sale; Take-Two does not disclose its actual rates, and retail codes and Rockstar's own store work differently.
  • "About thirty … about seventy" of $100: the same standard 30% console store rate (Epic Games v. Apple, N.D. Cal., 2021) applied to the $100 U.S. Ultimate Edition, before taxes. Our math.
  • "Ten days before launch": Nov. 9 to Nov. 19, 2026. "Three days before launch": guilty plea Nov. 16. "Almost five months early": pre-orders opened June 25, 2026.
  • "About eight percent": Take-Two closing price $252.40 (Nov. 6, 2025) to $232.00 (Nov. 7, 2025), the day after the second delay.
