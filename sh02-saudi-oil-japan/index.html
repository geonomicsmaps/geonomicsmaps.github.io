Why Japan Lends Its Oil Tanks to Saudi Arabia

Geonomics. Every narrated claim with its source and a short quote in the original language. Errors? Say so in the video's comments; corrections are pinned.

Narration

Japan lends Saudi Arabia oil tanks on Okinawa, rent-free.

  • Reuters via Hellenic Shipping News (JOGMEC statement); Argaam (Nov renewal), 2022, 2025
    In return for providing free storage space to Saudi Aramco Japan gets a priority claim on the oil stocks in an emergency

And it just agreed to triple that space for now, from 8 to as much as 24 million barrels. Why?

  • Kyodo News via The Mainichi (METI announcement), Nov 20; Saudi Press Agency via Zawya, 2026
    triple the amount of crude oil stored in Japan to a maximum of 24 million barrels

Japan gets over 90% of its crude from the Middle East, and most of it squeezes through one narrow strait: Hormuz.

  • Argus (Japanese government figures), 2025
    Japan has an exceptionally high dependence on the Middle East for oil compared with other countries
  • Argus citing METI data, 2025

Saudi Aramco gets a warehouse next to its customers in East Asia.

  • Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (METI), Oil Stockpiling Status, Jun, 2026
    当該社が東アジア向けの中継・備蓄基地として利用しつつ、我が国への原油供給が不足する際には、当該原油タンクの在庫を我が国向けに優先供給する事業

The price of free: if Japan runs short, Japan gets the first right to buy that oil.

  • Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (METI), Oil Stockpiling Status, Jun, 2026
    当該社が東アジア向けの中継・備蓄基地として利用しつつ、我が国への原油供給が不足する際には、当該原油タンクの在庫を我が国向けに優先供給する事業
  • Reuters via Hellenic Shipping News (JOGMEC statement); Argaam (Nov renewal), 2022, 2025
    In return for providing free storage space to Saudi Aramco Japan gets a priority claim on the oil stocks in an emergency

And it's not just Saudi Arabia. The UAE and Kuwait use Japanese tanks too.

  • Kyodo News via The Mainichi, Nov 20, 2026
    Oil companies from Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Kuwait lease storage tanks in Japan

In March, that was tested. Tankers effectively couldn't get through Hormuz.

  • METI news release, Mar 24, 2026
    crude oil tankers have been unable to effectively pass through the Strait of Hormuz for a prolonged period

At the time, producers had about 13 million barrels stored in Japan, roughly a week of its oil.

  • Reuters via The Standard (Hong Kong), Mar, 2026
    some 13 million barrels, or a total of seven days of supply, are jointly held in Japan by Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Kuwait

On March 26th, Japan began releasing its reserves, including the oil producers keep stored there.

  • Agency for Natural Resources and Energy (METI), Oil Stockpiling Status, Jun, note 3, 2026
    令和８年３月２６日以降、国家備蓄及び産油国共同備蓄の放出を開始

So the oil is Saudi, the tanks are Japanese, and in a crisis, Japan buys first.

Our calculations

  • "Triple, from 8 to as much as 24 million barrels": the existing Okinawa arrangement (about 1.3 million kl, roughly 8 million barrels) plus up to 16 million barrels of added space, as announced by Japan's trade ministry on Nov. 20, 2026.
  • "Roughly a week of its oil": the source's own figure ("some 13 million barrels, or a total of seven days of supply"), not our calculation.

Worth knowing

  • The added 16 million barrels of space is for a limited period and sits in Japan's national stockpile facilities; the 24 million figure is a maximum, not the amount stored today.
  • Japan's right is a priority right to purchase the stored crude in a shortage, not ownership of it.
  • Japan's trade ministry bulletin of Nov. 20, 2026 could not be opened directly when we checked; the announcement is cited through Kyodo News and the Saudi Press Agency.
  • "Over 90%" rounds the 2025 share (about 94%); "most of it" stands for METI data that about 93% of that Middle East crude passes through Hormuz.
